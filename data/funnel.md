# Funnel data

How our production onboarding converts, over the last 30 days. Rounded to the
nearest 5%, so treat these as the shape of the funnel rather than exact figures.

**North Star:** sign-up → engaged in the first session, which we define as
connected a source **and** reached the board. Those are the two ★ rows below.

## Step by step

Each row is the share of the people who reached the **previous** step.

| Step | Reach it |
|---|---|
| Signs up | — |
| Reaches the welcome flow | 100% |
| Finishes name + survey | 85% |
| Picks their tools | 80% |
| Picks what is slowing them down | 95% |
| ★ Connects a source | 30% |
| ★ Lands on the inbox board | 75% |
| Gets a 2nd Brain generated | 35% |

About 65% of people who sign up make it as far as the connect step.

End to end, out of everyone who signs up: **20%** connect a source, **15%** reach
the board, **5%** end up with a brain.
